Radiance Revolution

Comic/Manga Title in the works, taking place in some magical place, where the one above all passed down the Radiance to the inhabitant of the world, creating greed and power leading to Revolution

The project is planned to focuse on intense action, adventure with some elements of horror, comedy and strongly focuses on single person narritive. The art style is planned to be mostly black and white general comic style and a traditional procedure

CURRENT PLOT : As the Radiance fading into darkness, the goverment has announced to compelety eliminate any existance of the radiance. This decision has affected the whole country and the people, leading to a rebelious group trying to overcome the goverment (this plot is still in progress)

PROJECT GOALS

  • Finishing the story with relevent lore till the end of 2028
  • Develop a project progress document
  • Development a unique art style along the way
  • create a story I want to read
