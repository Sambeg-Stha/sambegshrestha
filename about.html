Ever since 2020, I have had a keen interest in making games. I was inspired by an indie game developer, "DANI", who showed me the fun in creating and making games. I worked in the game development field up until 2022 using the Unity Game Engine, then took a gap, and started again this year.

I am currently working on the next title, "The Eclips Project" — a collection of horror/action games following the same story world. Right now I am working on the first installment, Hauling Garbage.

In the future, I plan to continue working in indie development, making all sorts of games for consoles and mobile apps.

Computers have always been an interest of mine, from the time I had an old PC to play games. After 2020 I started programming, and now I am pursuing Computer Engineering and learning to code.

My current focus is on system-level programming using C and Rust, and memory management.

Ever since I was a kid, I loved to draw, from random scribbles to now having about 10 completely filled sketchbooks and many mini projects (paintings, DIY crafts and more).

I am really fond of comics and manga, and how they tell a story just by using still images and effects. I am currently working on creating my own comic with my own story idea, and plan to release it in early 2027.

Going forward, I plan to learn electronics — at least the basics of it — and develop some fun, practical projects.

At the present moment, the production time of these animations has increased from 4 videos a month to 1 video every 2 months; the inspiration and enjoyment are still there.
